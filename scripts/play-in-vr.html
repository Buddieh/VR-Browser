Play in VR

Drag this button onto Chromium's bookmarks bar (show the bar with Ctrl+Shift+B):

▶ Play in VR

Then, on any video page (YouTube and many other sites), click it. The first time, Chromium asks whether to open the link with the VR player; tick "always allow" and confirm.

If dragging doesn't work, add a bookmark by hand and paste this as its URL:

javascript:location.href='vrplay:'+encodeURIComponent(location.href)
